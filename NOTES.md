## Angle

Process bloat, not hallucination or apologising (the two clichés the rules
name). The roast is specific to how this agent actually works: every small
job gets folders, logs and a retrospective, and after all that ceremony the
one step that matters still needs the human's finger. The turn lands on the
last clause — the grand project was just a warm-up act for a single click.
